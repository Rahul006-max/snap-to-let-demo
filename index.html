How SnapToLet Works

See how to go from a physical sign to a verified digital listing in minutes.

Download SnapToLet

For Landlords

1

Generate your free To-Let signboard

Create a clean, bilingual To-Let signboard for each property using our free generator. Add your contact number on the To-Let signboard so tenants can easily reach you.

2

Place the signboard outside the property

Hang the signboard directly in front of the property where it is clearly visible from the street.

3

Snap or Scan your signboard with the app

Download the free SnapToLet app from Google Play or the App Store. Snap your signboard or Scan its QR code using the app to start listing the property.

4

Sign up and verify your account

Sign up as a Landlord with the phone number shown on your To-Let signboard and complete OTP verification. Once verified, your property appears instantly in your Landlord account.

5

Add more details to boost your listing

Upload photos and include key property details to help tenants decide quickly. Complete listings attract more interest and get rented faster.

6

Get more people to snap or scan your signboard

Invite your family, friends, or neighbours to Snap or Scan your To-Let signboard using the SnapToLet app. More scans help speed up verification and improve your property listing’s visibility.

For Tenants

1

Download the SnapToLet app

Get the free SnapToLet app from Google Play or the App Store to start finding available rental properties all across India.

2

Explore by location

Tap Explore to discover verified listings in any area. You can also use your current location to find nearby properties.

3

Snap your signboard with the app or Scan the QR code

Download the free SnapToLet app from Google Play or the App Store. Snap your signboard using the SnapToLet app or Scan its QR code using your phone's camera app to start listing your property.

4

Connect with Landlords and explore multiple options

Compare options, talk to Landlords and visit the properties you like to find your next home.

5

Help improve the renting experience for everyone

If you visited a property or spoke to a Landlord through SnapToLet, share quick feedback to help improve the renting experience for everyone.

6

Snap or Scan signboards to help the community

Snap or Scan To-Let signboards whenever you spot them to help grow listings and strengthen the renting community.

For Everyone

1

Download the SnapToLet app

Get the free SnapToLet app from Google Play or the App Store and become part of a growing movement to make renting faster, smarter and simpler across India.

2

Snap legitimate To-Let signboards outside vacant properties

3

Snap all genuine To-Let signboards around you

Don’t limit yourself to signboards created with the SnapToLet generator. Snap any authentic To-Let signboard outside a vacant property to help grow a trusted renting community across India.

5

Spread the word

If you know someone renting out their property or looking for a new home, let them know about SnapToLet. Every signboard you Snap and every person you tell helps make renting simpler and more reliable for everyone.

6

Be part of the real-estate revolution

Every Snap helps make renting simpler, safer and more transparent for everyone. Together, we can modernize how India finds homes.
